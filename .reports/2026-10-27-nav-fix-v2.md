## 1. Headline

Three separate things were happening. Two are fixed in this repo. The third is
not in this repo and is almost certainly the one you noticed most.

1. **A 3px vertical jump** between home/about and every other page, in a width
   band that moves with the language (820–980px). `Jorge Aguilar` wrapped onto
   two lines when the row got tight. **Fixed.**
2. **The mobile menu listed About before Work** on all 28 inner pages, against
   the desktop order, and the only Contact affordance there was the CTA at the
   bottom of the list. **Fixed.**
3. **`/motion/` has a 70px nav against the portfolio's 85px.** Clicking "Motion"
   from any page moves the bar **15px on desktop and 31px on mobile**. That page
   is served by a different project and this repo cannot fix it. **Flagged, see
   §7.** Its nav is also the only one on the whole site with a plain `Contact`
   link sitting at the end of the link list, which matches your second report
   literally.

The day-26 fix was not undone. It held at 1440px and 390px, which is where it
was verified. Both bugs below live at widths that audit did not sample.

padding, body margin/padding, brand offset, first-section top, document scroll

touches the nav.

### 2.2 Divergences found

**D1 — brand wraps, bar grows 3px (the reported shift).**
`.nav-brand` had no `white-space:nowrap`, while `.back-link` on the inner pages
did. On the six brand pages (`/`, `/about/` ×3 locales) the row is brand + five
links + language pills + CV + CTA with `flex-wrap:nowrap` on the container. When
it ran out of room, `Jorge Aguilar` broke onto two lines and the bar went
85 → 88px. The inner pages never hit it: their `.nav-pagelink` items hide at
1100px, so their row is never crowded.

The band depends on label width, which is why it looked random:

| page | 88px band (before) |
|---|---|
| `/de/`, `/de/about/` | 820–980px |
| `/es/`, `/es/about/` | 920–940px |
| `/`, `/about/` | 920px |

`before-de-860.png` shows it: `Jorge / Aguilar` on two lines.
`before-work-860.png` is the same width on `/work/` at 85px. Navigating between
the two moves the bar 3px.

Contributing: the long CV label (`Download CV` / `Lebenslauf` / `Currículum`)
only shrank to `CV` at 900px, which is below where the row actually runs out of
room once the five page links are counted.

**D2 — mobile menu order, and Contact at the end.**
The two variants are different components. Home/about use a numbered
`.mobile-menu-links` list; the 28 inner pages have no such list and put the page
links into `.mobile-menu-actions` as pill buttons.

| | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| home/about | Home 01 · Work 02 · About 03 · Motion 04 · Framework 05 · **Contact 06** · Get in touch · CV | unchanged |
| 28 inner pages | **About · Work** · Motion · Framework · Get in touch · CV | **Work · About** · Motion · Framework · Get in touch · CV |
| desktop, every page | Work · About · Motion · Framework · lang · CV · Get in touch | unchanged |

So mobile contradicted desktop on 28 pages, and Contact only exists as a named
list item on home/about — everywhere else it is the CTA at the bottom. With Work
first, that CTA now lands directly after Framework, which is the Contact slot in
the home/about list.

**D3 — the three privacy pages shipped one copy of the same broken menu.**
`/privacy/`, `/datenschutz/` and `/es/privacidad/` all carried the mobile menu
copied from `/datenschutz/`:

- its language switch pointed **all three locales at `/datenschutz/`** and marked
  DE current, so from `/privacy/` and `/es/privacidad/` the switch could not
  reach the other two translations (each page's **desktop** switch was correct);
- `/privacy/` (`lang="en"`) and `/es/privacidad/` (`lang="es"`) carried German
  interface strings — `Seitenmenü`, `Menü schließen`, `Sprache`, and a German
  `nav-toggle` aria-label;
- `/es/privacidad/` pointed its mobile page links at the **English** pages and
  labelled the CV `Descargar CV` against `Currículum` on every other ES page.

**D4 — 404 is 81px on a touch screen, 73px everywhere else.**
The 404s are the only pages that keep the language switch in the bar on mobile,
having no hamburger to hold it. On coarse pointers the pills grow to the 44px
minimum, and the capsule's own 1px border plus 3px padding push the row to 52px,
giving 14 + 52 + 14 + 1 = 81px. Desktop was unaffected (pills are 36px there).

**D5 — the CTA was clipped between 810 and 840px in German.**
`body{overflow-x:hidden}` hides the overflow rather than scrolling it, so
`Kontakt aufnehmen` was cut off at the right edge rather than reachable:

| page | clipped band (before) | document width |
|---|---|---|
| `/de/`, `/de/about/` | 810–840px | 850 vs 810–840 viewport |
| `/de/work/`, `/de/leadership-framework/`, `/de/wolt/` (all DE inner) | 810–830px | 837 |

Pre-existing on the DE inner pages. On the brand pages it was hidden *by* D1:
the brand wrapped instead of overflowing. Fixing D1 without this would have
converted a silent wrap into visible clipping.

**D6 — the CSS was 37 inline copies plus `contact/form.css`.**
Grouping pages by their nav-CSS signature gave **6 distinct variants** for what
is meant to be two designs. The two largest groups (23 pages) differed only in
the bar's background opacity and blur. This is the mechanism behind both this
bug and last week's: one copy drifts and nothing catches it.

### 2.3 What was already correct, and was not touched

- `position:sticky; top:0; z-index:20` on all 37 pages, at every width.
- Offset at load `0` on all 37 pages, both viewports — nothing above the nav.
  The `.scroll-progress` bar that precedes it in the DOM on 21 pages is
  `position:fixed` and out of flow.
- `body` margin-top and padding-top `0` everywhere.
- **Hero top padding: already consistent.** The first section sits flush against
  the bar on every page (hero top == nav height at 1440px, see the table). The
  brief expected a possible divergence here; there was none, so nothing was
  changed. The visible difference between pages is hero *content* (`h1` lands
  between 177px and 380px depending on whether there is an eyebrow label), not
  padding.
- Desktop link order was already identical on all 37 pages.
- `/nav.js` loads on all 37, `defer`, in `<head>`.

## 4. Changes applied

### 4.1 `e6f2971` — markup order (28 + 3 files)

- 28 inner pages: swapped About/Work in `.mobile-menu-actions`.
- `/privacy/`, `/datenschutz/`, `/es/privacidad/`: mobile menu rebuilt per page
  from that page's own desktop nav — correct language switch
  (`en=/privacy/ de=/datenschutz/ es=/es/privacidad/` with the right current
  flag), localized interface strings and aria-labels, and ES links/labels on
  `/es/privacidad/` including `Descargar CV` → `Currículum` (desktop too).

No CSS, no geometry.

### 4.2 `bf60978` — new `/nav.css`, −421 lines of duplicated CSS (39 files)

`/nav.css` is loaded **last in `<head>`** on all 37 pages with a nav, so the
contract wins over anything a page still declares. It owns: `position`/`top`/
`z-index`, vertical padding, `border-bottom`, `.nav-inner`, the compact
breakpoint, the optional-label breakpoint, and the auto-hide transition driven
by `/nav.js`. Those declarations were deleted from all 37 pages and from
`contact/form.css`.

Appearance stays per page on purpose: background, blur, typography and the
mobile overlay are untouched, so the case studies keep their darker,
stronger-blur bar.

Two horizontal values were unified where copies disagreed:

- `.nav-inner` gains `gap:16px` on the 6 brand pages (the 31 others had it).
- The side gutter below the compact breakpoint is 20px everywhere. The brand
  pages used `var(--gutter)`, which is 32px down to 640px, so between 641 and
  800px their bar had a 32px gutter against everyone else's 20px.

### 4.3 `1af1860` — the geometry fixes (33 files)

- **D1:** `.nav-brand, .back-link { white-space: nowrap }` in `nav.css`, and the
  long CV label now shortens at **1000px** instead of 900px.
- **D5:** the hamburger breakpoint moves **800 → 900px** on the 32 pages that
  have one, with `nav.css`'s compact padding in lockstep. Above 900px the full
  row fits in all three languages; below it, the menu holds the links instead of
  the bar clipping them.
- **D4:** `nav.css` drops the language capsule's border and padding *inside the
  bar* below 900px. The 44px touch target is unchanged; only the pill outline
  goes, and only on the 404s, the single pages that show it there.

**Behaviour change worth knowing:** between 801 and 900px you now get the
hamburger instead of the full row. That band is exactly where the row did not
fit. It is uniform across all 32 pages.

## 6. Deviations from the brief

- **The two nav variants were not merged into one.** The brief asked for the
  same structure everywhere, but merging would mean porting the numbered
  `.mobile-menu-links` component and about twenty CSS rules onto 28 pages and
  giving the inner pages a `Home` item — a redesign of the mobile menu, which
  the brief rules out. What was made identical is the geometry contract and the
  link order. The remaining structural difference is listed in §7.
- **`.nav-css` owns geometry only, not appearance.** Full extraction would have
  had to reconcile `!important` usage and two genuinely different designs across
  37 files, with no benefit to the reported bug. Background and blur stay per
  page, so the case studies keep their look.
- **Hero top padding needed no change** — it was already consistent (§2.3).
- **`/impressum/` language switch left alone.** All three of its locales point at
  `/impressum/` because there is only a German imprint. Desktop and mobile agree,
  so it is consistent, just a one-language page.

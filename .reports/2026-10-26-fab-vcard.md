# Report: Floating QR vCard Button (FAB)

**Date:** 2026-10-26
**Task:** FAB flotante que abre el modal QR vCard desde cualquier página
**Session:** Claude Code
**Status:** SHIPPED (real-device and GA4 Realtime checks pending, see §7)
**Commit:** 96fde0f (feature) · report finalized in the follow-up commit

## 1. Context

The vCard modal shipped in `4b7e45c` only opened from `/contact/`. The real use case is in person: someone asks for Jorge's contact, he takes out his phone, taps once, shows the QR, they scan it. That needs a trigger on every page.

This task adds a floating action button (FAB, bottom-right) on all portfolio pages that opens the **same** modal. The modal moved from `/contact/` into one global component.

Scope: the 37 portfolio pages, EN/DE/ES. Excluded: `/motion/` and `/343ride-landing.html`. `/cv/` is also excluded, see §6.

## 2. Diagnóstico (starting state)

- **The modal lived only on `/contact/`:**
  - inline `<dialog>` markup in each of the 3 contact pages (3 copies)
  - its logic in `/contact/vcard.js`
  - its CSS in `/contact/form.css`, using that file's variables (`--field`, `--border`, …)

  The other pages have no shared stylesheet, and each names its CSS variables differently. So the modal couldn't be reused as-is: it needed its own self-contained styles, the way `consent.js` injects its banner CSS.
- **Cookie banner:**
  - `consent.js` appends `.cc-banner` (fixed bottom, full width) and `.cc-veil` to `<body>` in `show()`, and removes them in `close()`.
  - `show()` runs on load when there's no stored decision, and again from the Datenschutz "Einstellung ändern" control.
  - Before this change there was no public signal of the banner's state.
- **Script order:** `consent.js` comes before `nav.js` in `<head>` on all 37 pages (checked). Both are `defer`, so a script added after `nav.js` runs after `consent.js`.
- **Stacking:** nav z 20, case-study reading rail z 40, mobile menu overlay z 60, cookie banner z 2147483647, modal `<dialog>` in the top layer.
- **Footers:** on 28 of 74 page/viewport combinations, a FAB at the specified position (24/24 desktop, 16/16 mobile) covers the footer's Privacy/Datenschutz link once the page is scrolled to the bottom (measured with `elementsFromPoint`, before the fix in §3).

## 3. Changes applied

### Files created:
- **`/vcard.js`**: the only home of the modal now. Self-contained, like `consent.js`:
  - Injects its CSS (`#vc-style`, own `--vc-*` tokens).
  - Builds the `<dialog id="vcard-modal">` with trilingual copy (language from the URL path, as for the page itself).
  - Creates the FAB (`<button class="vc-fab">`) and binds every `.vcard-open` on the page (the existing "Save my contact" button on `/contact/`).
  - Sends the analytics events (§4).
  - Skips `/motion/` paths. Does nothing in browsers without `<dialog>`: the contact-page button is a real link to the `.vcf` and still downloads it.
- `.reports/2026-10-26-fab-vcard.md` (this file) and `.reports/2026-10-26-fab-vcard/*.png`

### Files modified:
- **`consent.js`** (2 lines plus a comment): `show()` sets `data-cc-banner="open"` on `<html>`, and `close()` removes it. That's the coordination hook; no consent logic changed.
- **All 37 portfolio pages:** `<script src="/vcard.js" defer></script>` right after `nav.js`.
- **`contact/index.html`, `de/contact/index.html`, `es/contact/index.html`:** removed the inline `<dialog>` and the `/contact/vcard.js` script tag. The "Save my contact" button stays as it was.
- **`contact/form.css`:** removed the modal CSS (now in `/vcard.js`). Kept the page's own button styles (`.vcard-cta`, `.vcard-open`) and added its focus ring.
- **`datenschutz/index.html`, `privacy/index.html`, `es/privacidad/index.html`:** the section 14a sentence about Umami events now says "the website" (not "the contact page") and lists exactly what is sent: event name, page language, page type, and whether it came from the contact page or the floating button. The previous wording said "event name and page language, nothing else", but `page_type` was already being sent, so this also fixes that.

### Files deleted:
- `contact/vcard.js` (logic merged into `/vcard.js`).

### Design / CSS:
- **FAB:**
  - Circle, 56×56 on desktop (24px from right and bottom) and 48×48 at ≤800px (16px). Both are at least 44×44.
  - Lime `#B8FF3D` with a subtle 1px dark border and shadow `0 4px 12px rgba(0,0,0,.3)`.
  - 22px/20px QR glyph (3 finder squares plus modules) in `#0B0B0D`.
  - Hover (pointer devices only) `scale(1.05)` + `brightness(1.06)`. Active `scale(.95)`. Transitions `.2s ease`, fade `.3s`.
  - `:focus-visible`: 2px lime outline with 3px offset plus a soft lime glow ring.
  - Hidden in print. Respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **Safe area:** `right`/`bottom` add `env(safe-area-inset-right/bottom, 0px)`.
- **Cookie banner:** `html[data-cc-banner] .vc-fab { opacity:0; visibility:hidden; pointer-events:none }`. Pure CSS, so it doesn't depend on script timing. When the banner closes (Accept or Decline), the attribute goes away and the FAB fades in over 0.3s. With a stored decision the banner never opens, so the FAB is visible from the first paint of `vcard.js`. If the banner is reopened (Datenschutz control), the FAB hides again until the new decision.
- **Mobile menu:** `body.menu-open .vc-fab` is hidden as well. The overlay is above it (z 60 > 50) anyway.
- **Footer lift:** when the page's last `<footer>` scrolls into view, the FAB's `bottom` increases by the visible height of the footer (`--vc-lift`, updated once per frame on scroll and resize). It never covers the Impressum or Datenschutz links.
- **Modal on phones, by source:**
  - From the FAB, Jorge is showing the QR, so the QR is the focus (up to 320px) and the `.vcf` link stays a quiet text link.
  - From `/contact/`, the visitor is on their own phone and can't scan their own screen, so the `.vcf` link becomes the lime full-width button (as in `4b7e45c`).

  Implemented with `data-source` on the `<dialog>`.

## 4. Analytics

| Trigger | Event | Params |
|---|---|---|
| FAB (any page) | `vcard_modal_open` | `source: 'fab'`, `page_language`, `page_type` (home / about / work / framework / case_study / contact / other) |
| `/contact/` button | `vcard_modal_open` | `source: 'contact_page'`, `page_language`, `page_type: 'contact'` |
| `.vcf` link in modal | `vcard_download` | `source` of the trigger that opened the modal, `page_language`, `page_type` |

- GA4 goes through `window.__consent.track` (which adds `page_type` itself and is gated on consent). Umami goes through `umami.track` with the same parameters, including `page_type`.
- Both are sent **only if `__consent.granted()`**. Without consent, or with `?noga=1`, nothing is sent.
- `vcard_download` now also carries `source`. That wasn't requested, but it tells you which path leads to a saved contact, not just an opened modal.

## 5. Verification

Local, in headless Chrome (puppeteer-core 24.10.0) against the repo served locally, with Umami stubbed and GA read from `dataLayer`:

### Cookie banner coordination
- New visitor, home, 1440px: banner shown, `data-cc-banner` set, FAB `visibility:hidden`, opacity 0. Screenshot `A-banner-1440.png`.
- Accept: FAB visible (opacity 1) after the 0.3s fade.
- New visitor, `/about/`, Decline: the FAB also appears. Clicking it opens the modal, and **no events** are sent (no consent).
- Stored decision: FAB visible at `domcontentloaded` + 80 ms, no banner.
- `/datenschutz/` "Einstellung ändern" reopens the banner: FAB hidden. Deciding again brings it back.

### FAB → modal
- Click FAB → `#vcard-modal` open, `data-source="fab"`. **Exactly one** `<dialog>` on the page. Escape closes, focus returns to the FAB, and the FAB stays visible. Screenshots `A-modal-fab-1440.png`, `E-modal-fab-390.png`.
- Keyboard: Tab reaches the FAB (15 tabs on `/contact/` at 1440px, last in the tab order). Enter opens the modal, Escape closes, Space opens.
- Events with consent: `vcard_modal_open {page_language:'en', source:'fab', page_type:'home'}` to GA4 and Umami.

### /contact/ flow (not broken)
- "Save my contact" opens the same global modal with `data-source="contact_page"`. The download fires `vcard_download {source:'contact_page'}` to GA4 and Umami. The × button closes the modal and returns focus to the button. On phones the `.vcf` link is the lime button (`E-modal-contact-390.png`).

### All pages
- 37 pages × 1440px and 390px = 74 combinations: the FAB is present and visible, 56px/48px, at 24/24 and 16/16 from the edges. The exceptions are the 3 one-screen 404 pages, where the footer is always in view and the FAB sits just above it, as designed.
- Scrolled to the bottom of every page: **0** links or buttons under the FAB (it was 28 combinations before the footer lift).
- Case study at 1440px: the FAB sits clear of the 3px reading rail on the right edge (`E-wolt-1440.png`). At 390px the rail is a 2px bar at the top, so they don't meet (`E-wolt-390.png`).
- Mobile menu open (`/wolt/`, 390px): FAB hidden. Closed: FAB visible again.
- The nav's auto-hide is unaffected (the FAB is a separate fixed element; `nav.js` is unchanged).

## 6. Deviations from spec

- **`/cv/` gets no FAB.** It's not deployed (`cv/` is in `.assetsignore`, so the live site returns 404), and `cv/index.html` is the source that `npm run cv` prints to `Jorge_Aguilar_CV.pdf`. A floating button there would end up in the PDF.
- **Native `<button>` instead of `role="button"` + `tabindex="0"`:** a `<button>` has both built in, and Enter/Space activation, without extra code. Same result for assistive tech.
- **Footer lift (not in the brief):** at the specified fixed position, the FAB covered the footer's Privacy/Datenschutz link on 28 of 74 page/viewport combinations. The legal links must stay reachable, so the FAB moves up while the footer is in view.
- **The modal changes slightly by trigger on phones** (§3): QR-first from the FAB, download-first from `/contact/`. The `4b7e45c` modal always made the download the primary action on phones, which is wrong for the "show my QR" use case.
- **`consent.js` changed (2 lines):** the brief asked for an observer or listener on `consent.js`. A DOM attribute plus CSS is simpler and has no load-order race. Consent logic is untouched.
- **`vcard_download` gains `source`.**
- **Privacy sentence 14a reworded** in DE/EN/ES (§3). It also corrects an omission from the previous commit (`page_type` was already sent but not listed).

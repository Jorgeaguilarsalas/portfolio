1. Privacy at a glance

This website is essentially an information site. It additionally offers a contact form (section 12) and uses audience measurement with Google Analytics 4 (section 13). Cookies and consent. Without your consent no analytics cookies are set and you are not recognised. The second measurement described in section 14 does not depend on that decision: it works without cookies and without recognition and therefore also runs if you decline in the banner. You can decline without any restriction on your use of the site, and you can change your decision at any time.

14. Audience measurement with Cloudflare Web Analytics

This website additionally uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, a web analytics service provided by Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA, represented for users in the European Economic Area by Cloudflare Germany GmbH, Rosental 7, 80331 Munich. The measurement is not limited to particular areas: it covers all pages of this website including the publication „Operations in Motion“ (/motion/, /de/motion/ and /es/motion/) as well as the subdomains of jorgeag.com. The measurement script is not delivered from our server but inserted into the page automatically by Cloudflare while the page is delivered to your browser via the CDN.

No cookies, no recognition, no consent. Cloudflare Web Analytics sets no cookies, uses no browser storage (localStorage) and places no identifier on your device. Neither a visitor nor a session identifier is assigned, and no „fingerprinting“ via IP address, user agent or other characteristics takes place. Since no information is therefore stored on or read from your device, § 25(1) TDDDG does not apply and no consent is obtained. This measurement is consequently independent of your decision in the cookie banner: declining there does not switch it off, and it records all views of this website.

Data processed. When a page of this website is opened, your browser loads a measurement script from static.cloudflareinsights.com and then transmits the measured values to the address jorgeag.com/cdn-cgi/rum, that is to our own domain, from where Cloudflare receives them. What is transmitted is in particular: the address requested, the previously visited page (referrer), the time of the request, information on browser, operating system and device type from the user agent, the country derived from the IP address, and technical measurements of the page load time. Your IP address is processed for the transmission and to derive the country; it is removed as early as the nearest Cloudflare data centre and is not carried over into the analysis.

What the report shows. The analysis is aggregated. A „visit“ there is simply a page view whose referrer comes from another website; there is no „unique visitor“ in the sense of conventional analytics services, and your behaviour is not combined across several page views or visits.

Legal basis and objection. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in data-minimising, consent-free audience measurement that shows which contributions are read (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). You may object to this processing at any time under Art. 21 GDPR using the contact routes named in section 3. Since there is no banner control, you can also block the measurement script technically, for example by using a browser extension to block scripts from static.cloudflareinsights.com; the pages remain fully usable.
